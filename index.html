Case Study 01

Double Points Campaign: Measuring Incremental Member Spend

Campaign Effectiveness Incrementality Control-Group Lift
Commercial Lift: Campaign vs Control Baseline
Sanitized uplift in spend per member per day and basket size (AOV)
01 — Business Challenge:

Does a Double Points incentive mechanic meaningfully stimulate incremental spending, or does it merely award extra points for organic transactions that would have happened anyway?

02 — Data & Method:

Analyzed transactional telemetry across campaign dates. Benchmarked participating members against an unexposed control cohort over equivalent trading days to isolate true behavioral lift.

03 — Analysis & Key Findings:
  • Achieved a ~40% increase in spend per member per day during the promotional window.
  • Drove a ~30% uplift in Average Order Value (AOV), proving basket-stretching behavior.
04 — Key Insight:

Double Points acts as an immediate spending multiplier rather than just a visit shifter, creating high basket expansion without permanent gross margin erosion.

05 — Recommendation:

Deploy Double Points selectively during seasonal off-peak trading windows or for slower-performing units where incremental margin comfortably exceeds the liability of future point redemptions.

06 — Impact & Measurement:

Evaluated through net incremental revenue minus point liability, confirming positive commercial ROI over baseline control groups.

Case Study 02

Voucher Campaign Analysis: From Campaign Conversion to ROI

Voucher Economics 5.8x ROAS Audience Targeting
Voucher Telemetry Funnel Progression
Sanitized funnel: Assigned members to physical in-store burn
01 — Business Challenge:

Stimulate customer spend through targeted voucher injections while maintaining strict unit economics and avoiding discount waste on organic returners.

02 — Data & Method:

Mapped campaign funnel conversion: assigned members → transacting members → voucher redeemers. Evaluated spend-to-cost return across cash vouchers versus item-based promotions.

03 — Analysis & Key Findings:
  • Direct injected vouchers yielded a sustainable ~5.8x spend-to-cost ratio among active redeemers.
  • ~68% of total voucher-driven spending was generated by lapsed members (inactive for 90+ days).
  • Cash-denominated vouchers triggered higher basket stretch than free item promos, which suffered from basket compression.
04 — Key Insight:

Fixed cash vouchers create superior spend expansion over free items, provided segmentation models exclude members who already transact frequently.

05 — Recommendation:

Refine distribution rules: enforce minimum spend thresholds and prioritize injection toward at-risk and dormant cohorts with high historical lifetime value.

06 — Impact & Measurement:

Tracked via net incremental gross margin and burn velocity, safeguarding outlet profitability.

Case Study 03

Retention & Repeat Transaction: Understanding the Customer Lifecycle

Cohort Diagnostics The 44-Day Cliff Lifecycle Automation
Cohort Retention Curve Across Transactions
Sanitized retention drop-off showing the steep Transaction 1 → 2 cliff
01 — Business Challenge:

Diagnose where newly acquired members drop off along the customer lifecycle and identify optimal windows for automated CRM interventions.

02 — Data & Method:

Conducted longitudinal cohort analysis across newly acquired members, tracking transaction progression milestones and calculating median elapsed days between visits.

03 — Analysis & Key Findings:
  • Retention drops steeply from 1st transaction (~58%) down to 2nd transaction (~32%).
  • The median gap between Visit 1 and Visit 2 was 44 days (the primary churn hazard), but contracted sharply to 12 days once habituation formed at Visit 3.
04 — Key Insight:

The window between Day 14 and Day 30 is the critical inflection period. Uncontacted users past Day 30 face an exponential increase in permanent churn.

05 — Recommendation:

Implement automated Day-14 re-engagement journeys featuring time-sensitive incentives for unreturned members, with holdout control groups to measure true incremental repeat visits.

06 — Impact & Measurement:

Measured through compression of median days between initial visits and overall repeat purchase conversion lift.

Case Study 04

Communication: WhatsApp vs Email Conversion Benchmark

Channel Telemetry Full-Funnel Burn Channel Economics
Full-Funnel Channel Benchmark (%)
Sanitized performance: Open → Click/Tap → In-Store Burn
01 — Business Challenge:

Evaluate the commercial effectiveness of CRM delivery channels beyond vanity metrics (open rates), measuring actual in-store voucher redemption and revenue contribution.

02 — Data & Method:

Audited end-to-end telemetry across direct channels (WhatsApp Blasts, App Push Notifications, and Email Newsletters) tracking delivery to physical POS burn.

03 — Analysis & Key Findings:
Channel Read / Open Click / Action In-Store Burn
WhatsApp Direct ~43.7% ~35.4% ~13.9%
Push Notification ~18.2% ~8.6% ~6.8%
Email Marketing ~14.5% ~3.1% ~2.4%

WhatsApp contributed ~69% of all redeemed vouchers, driving immediate footfall velocity compared to flat day-of-blast email performance.

04 — Key Insight:

While WhatsApp has a higher per-message dispatch cost, its 5.8x higher burn rate over email yields superior cost-efficiency for high-tier member re-engagement.

05 — Recommendation:

Adopt a tiered multi-channel dispatch strategy: reserve WhatsApp for high-value VIP segments and time-sensitive voucher injections; leverage Push for automated H-7 expiry triggers, and use Email for low-cost brand announcements.

06 — Impact & Measurement:

Audited through campaign delivery ROI and message-to-redemption conversion rates.

Operational Problem Solving

POS SKU Mapping Discrepancy & Mitigation SOP

Root-Cause Analysis

The Incident: During a targeted product campaign, customer purchases of qualifying items failed to trigger completed status in the loyalty rules engine.

Investigation & Root Cause: Audited POS transactional logs against CRM event records and identified an unmapped item code (SKU mismatch) introduced during a recent POS menu update.

Business Impact: Identified early before widespread campaign launch, preventing loyalty breakage and customer service escalation.

Action & SOP: Executed manual benefit reconciliation for affected guests and instituted a cross-functional pre-flight SKU verification checklist between IT and Marketing prior to all future campaign deployments.
